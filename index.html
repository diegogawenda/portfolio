03 Selected work

Real problems, measurable results.

Case study — Flex

From monthly to weekly releases at fintech speed

Challenge: A rent-payment fintech shipping weekly, with mobile regression eating days before every release.

Approach: Designed and integrated a mobile automation suite directly into GitHub Actions, rebuilt the QA workload estimation process, and introduced shift-left checks earlier in development.

Outcome: Regression time cut 75%, unlocking daily release cycles instead of weekly, with 80% more predictable delivery forecasts.

75% faster regression → daily releases
Case study — Almanac

Automation built from zero

Challenge: An async collaboration platform relying entirely on manual regression testing, slowing every release.

Approach: Built an end-to-end Playwright/TypeScript automation framework from scratch and embedded automated checks earlier in the development pipeline.

Outcome: Team productivity improved 50%, with 20% fewer defects reaching QA.

0 → full E2E framework, 50% productivity gain
Case study — dLocal

Scaling quality across squads

Challenge: A fintech unicorn's growing automation suite was accumulating flaky, low-value tests, eroding trust in CI signal across multiple product squads.

Approach: Implemented daily flaky-test monitoring to identify and eliminate low-value tests, and led and coached a 12-engineer QA team through cross-team knowledge-transfer sessions.

Outcome: Automation ROI increased 80%, with 20% better cross-team efficiency.

80% automation ROI across 12 engineers

12 FAQ

Questions before we talk.

Who is this for?

Engineering teams that ship software and want testing to catch more problems with less friction. If you have no QA yet, an assessment is a good starting point. If you already have a team, I work alongside it.

Do I have to choose between the two plans?

No. Many teams start with an assessment and decide afterwards whether they want support while the plan is implemented. A partnership can also start directly if you already know your pain points.

How do we start?

With a 30-minute discovery call to understand your challenge and see if we are a good match. Nothing else is decided on that call.

Will this replace my QA team?

No. The goal is to strengthen it. I assess, plan and coach, and your team keeps ownership of the work.

Can we sign an NDA first?

Yes. I am happy to sign one before looking at anything internal.
